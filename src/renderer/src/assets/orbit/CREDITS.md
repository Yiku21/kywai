# Orbital world textures

Earth (day, night, clouds), Jupiter, Saturn and Sun:
Solar System Scope / INOVE, https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/.
Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.
Earth source files are 8K maps; day and night were resized to 4096 × 2048 and the
clouds to 2048 × 1024, all JPEG-compressed. The other maps were JPEG-compressed at
their original 2K size.
No endorsement is implied.

Moon: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit (2025 map),
Ernie Wright / LROC / LRO, https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/.
Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
Bundled at its original 2048 × 1024 size with JPEG recompression.

This is an artistic orbital environment. Distances, sizes and time are compressed
for legibility; planetary positions are not current ephemerides.
